# Product overview

**Status:** current scope page; fuller product narrative is owned by documentation Task 4.

MyoStat combines strength, nutrition, and bodyweight logging with deterministic, evidence-graded advice. It is aimed at lifters who want one local-first record and who value explicit uncertainty over confident coaching theatre.

The durable product promise and non-goals live in the [product contract](../../PRODUCT.md). Current implementation claims and gaps belong in [feature status](../reference/feature-status.md), while the [feature tour](feature-tour.md) describes the user-facing routes.
