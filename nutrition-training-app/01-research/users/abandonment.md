# Abandonment & Retention Reality

> **Status:** Phase 1 baseline. Wave 1 Stream C extends this — everything below is retained, not replaced.
> Verbatim Phase 1 source archived at `03-sources/raw-notes/phase1/constraints.md`.

Evidence grades: **[A]** meta-analysis/replicated RCT · **[B]** single good study/consistent observational · **[C]** mechanistic/small-n/expert consensus · **[D]** industry/anecdote.

## Phase 1 baseline

- Nutrition-app retention is brutal: day-30 commonly cited **~30–45%** for diet/nutrition apps, single-digit-to-25% for fitness apps broadly; **~70% abandon within 2 weeks** if logging feels heavy. **Grade [D]** — these came from industry blog aggregation, not primary sources. They are doing real argumentative work (they generate the payoff-latency objection to the Phase 1 recommendation) and therefore **need primary sourcing in Wave 1**.
- Logging burden is the most-cited churn driver [D].
- **KEY FINDING — approximate-sustained beats precise-abandoned [A/B]:** across **5 RCTs** of simplified vs detailed dietary self-monitoring, **4/5 showed equivalent weight loss**; a pilot RCT found near-equal 5% weight-loss rates (43.2% vs 42.9%) **and higher engagement in the simplified arm**. Detailed calorie-logging adherence *declines over time*, and non-loggers do not reach clinically meaningful outcomes.
- Product implication (high confidence): **build for approximate logging** — photo/quick-add, tier or portion buckets ("palm/fist", S/M/L), recent-foods, barcode. Precision is optional depth, never the default path. Chasing gram accuracy loses users, who then get zero benefit.

## Carried-forward open questions (from `00-meta/carryover.md` §2)

These are Wave 1 Stream C's primary targets, not optional extras:

1. **Primary sources for the retention curves.** Replace the [D] figures with real numbers, segmented where possible.
2. **Does one app beat two apps for adherence?** This is the load-bearing claim of the entire project after Phase 1 killed the computational-moat premise — and Phase 1 produced *zero* evidence for it. If false, the project premise fails again.
3. **Abandonment causes separated by segment** — Phase 1 treated "users" as monolithic.
4. **Do users actually fail to make the cross-app recomposition read?** Asserted from forum impression, never checked.
5. **Habit-formation literature applied to logging** — absent from Phase 1 entirely.
6. **Gamification evidence, including the case against** — extrinsic rewards undermining intrinsic motivation was never examined, despite Phase 1 killing streaks on ED grounds alone.
7. **Social features** — when they aid retention and when they drive attrition.
